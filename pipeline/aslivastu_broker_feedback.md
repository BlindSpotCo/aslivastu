# AsliVastu — User Feedback (Property Broker Perspective)

**Context:** Used AsliVastu day-to-day while showing properties and advising clients across South Delhi, Dwarka, Noida, and Ghaziabad. Mostly used it to back up pitches to buyers and occasionally pushed back on it when clients quoted a low score at me.

## What worked

Clients trust a number from a "neutral" source more than they trust me telling them a locality is good — even if I'm right. Having the NQI grade to show on my phone during a site visit closes the credibility gap faster than my usual pitch. The schools list is something I actively screenshot and send to clients with young kids — it's the single most-used feature for me. Air quality being sourced from CPCB (a government body) also lands well with NRI clients who don't trust random blog rankings.

## Where it fell short

**A low score costs me deals, and I can't argue with it because I can't see the methodology.** More than once a client has pulled up a "C+" for a PIN I know for a fact has improved a lot in the last year — new metro line, resurfaced roads — and I have no way to show them the score reflects 2023 data, not today. I need something I can point to and say "here's what changed since this was last scored."

**Doesn't match how I actually work.** I sell by locality and sometimes by specific society/complex, not by PIN code. A PIN code covering both a premium gated community and an older unauthorized colony gives both the same score, which undersells the good listings and oversells the bad ones. Clients notice this fast and it undermines trust in the whole tool, not just that one PIN.

**No way to generate a shareable report.** I want a clean one-page PDF or link with a property/locality's score that I can WhatsApp to a client or attach to a brochure — right now I'd have to screenshot the page, which looks unprofessional in a client deck.

**Crime and safety framing can be a dealbreaker with zero nuance.** A high crime count with no context (population, trend direction, type of crime) is exactly the kind of number a nervous buyer fixates on. I don't need the data hidden — I need it presented in a way I can actually explain, not just a scary raw total.

**No coverage indicator I can lead with.** Some PINs I sell in show up with partial data (missing power or infra scores) and the composite quietly reweights around it. I'd rather know upfront "this PIN only has 3 of 5 dimensions scored" so I'm not caught off guard mid-pitch when a client asks why the number looks different from a neighboring PIN.

**Nothing on price context.** A score without any connection to price-per-sqft trends is only half the picture for a buyer deciding where to invest. Even a rough directional note ("this area's infra score has been rising, prices have followed in comparable zones") would help me make the investment case, not just the livability case.

## Suggestions, in order of what would help most

1. Shareable, exportable report (PDF/link) per PIN or locality, branded lightly enough that I can send it to clients.
2. Society/colony-level scoring option where data supports it, not just PIN-wide.
3. A visible "last updated" date and a change-log or delta indicator per dimension, so I can explain if something's improved since the score was set.
4. Show data completeness/coverage per PIN clearly, not just folded into the composite.
5. Add context to crime numbers (rate, trend, category breakdown) instead of a bare total.
6. Any signal connecting the score to price trend — even directional — would make this a sales tool, not just a research tool.

## Bottom line

I use it and I'll keep using it — it's a fast, credible-looking way to back up what I already know about an area. But right now it's built for a buyer doing solo research, not for someone using it in front of a client. A few changes (shareable reports, freshness transparency, finer granularity) would turn it into something I'd actively recommend to other brokers, rather than something I quietly cross-check before repeating to a client.
