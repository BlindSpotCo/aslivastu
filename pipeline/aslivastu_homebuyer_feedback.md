# AsliVastu — User Feedback (Homebuyer Perspective)

**Context:** Used AsliVastu to shortlist neighborhoods across Delhi NCR before making a home purchase decision. Compared 6–7 PIN codes across South Delhi, Dwarka, and Noida.

## What worked

The single composite score (NQI) is genuinely useful as a first filter — being able to see an "A" vs a "C+" at a glance saved time I'd otherwise spend cross-referencing five different government sites. The schools section was the most valuable part for me personally: seeing an actual list of nearby CBSE/ICSE schools, not just a count, helped me narrow down Dwarka Sector 8 vs Sector 9 for my kids' school commute. Air quality being pulled from two sources (CPCB + WAQI) also gave me more confidence than a single-station reading would have.

## Where it fell short

**Data felt out of date and I couldn't tell how much.** Nothing on the page told me when the crime, power, or road-condition numbers were last refreshed. I later found out (informally) that this data comes from static reports, some from 2023 — that's a big deal for a decision this size, and it should be disclosed up front, not something I have to assume.

**PIN codes are too coarse for how I actually think about neighborhoods.** A single PIN in Delhi can span both a quiet residential pocket and a busy market road. I wanted a score for the specific colony or sector I was looking at, not the whole 110-square-km PIN catchment.

**No visibility into how the score is calculated.** I want to know why crime counts for 30% and schools only 10% — those aren't my priorities as a buyer with young kids. A way to re-weight the categories myself (or at least see the weighting) would make the score feel less like a black box.

**Missing dimensions I actually care about.** Water supply hours, road quality, and sewerage/waterlogging risk are all things the site clearly tracks (I found them buried in a "details" section) but they don't count toward the overall score. Waterlogging risk alone should be a dealbreaker filter during monsoon season — it feels wrong that it's invisible in the headline number.

**No side-by-side comparison view.** I ended up opening the same neighborhood in multiple browser tabs to compare two PIN codes. A comparison table would have been much faster.

**Crime numbers with no context.** "310 total cognizable crimes" means nothing to me without knowing the population of the area or a per-capita rate. Two areas could show similar totals with very different actual risk.

## Suggestions, in order of what would help most

1. Show a "data as of [date]" stamp per category, and flag which categories are static vs. live-updated.
2. Let users compare 2–3 PIN codes side by side.
3. Surface water/road/sewerage into the main score, or at minimum let me toggle them on.
4. Add a per-capita or per-1000-household crime rate instead of (or alongside) raw totals.
5. Support sub-PIN or colony-level granularity where data allows it.
6. Make the weighting adjustable, even with simple presets ("Family with kids," "Young professional," "Investor").

## Bottom line

Would use again for the first-pass shortlist — it's faster than manually checking eight government sources. But I wouldn't make a final decision on the NQI score alone without knowing how fresh the underlying data is, and I'd want the comparison and re-weighting features before recommending it to a friend.
